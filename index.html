How it works

Three swipes to a fair split

No spreadsheets, no mental math — the camera and a couple of swipes do it all at the table.

1

Scan the receipt

Point your camera at the bill — Bill Swiper reads every item and price automatically.

2

Swipe each dish to a guest

Drag the dish card into a guest's zone. Shared a starter? Send it to everyone with one tap.

3

Get the total, tip included

The app shows exactly who owes what, adds the tip, and hands out titles like "biggest spender" and "most modest."
